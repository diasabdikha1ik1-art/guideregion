# Figma layout guide — Guideregion

Create two frames and reproduce the design from the website.

## Frame 1: Desktop
- Frame: **1440 × 2400 px** (adjust height as needed).
- Layout grid: **12 columns**, margin **88 px**, gutter **24 px**.
- Header: 80 px high; logo left, nav centered/right, search on right.
- Hero: full width, about 700 px high; mountain image background with dark gradient; heading and CTA on the left.
- Intro: two-column section.
- Places: 3 equal cards in a 3-column grid.
- Route banner: dark green background, copy left, stylized route illustration right.
- Planner: two columns; explanatory text left and form card right.
- Footer: four columns.

## Frame 2: Mobile
- Frame: **390 × 3000 px** (adjust height as needed).
- Layout grid: **4 columns**, margin **20 px**, gutter **12 px**.
- Header: logo, compact search icon, burger menu.
- Hero: single column, large heading, stacked or wrapped CTAs.
- Intro, cards, route planner, and form: one column.
- Buttons and touch controls: at least **44 × 44 px** where practical and placed within easy thumb reach.
- Footer: two columns, then stacked on very narrow screens.

## Themes
Create Light and Dark versions (or use Figma variables):
- Light background `#F7F6F1`, cards `#FFFFFF`, text `#17251F`, green `#244D3B`, accent `#D8ED9C`.
- Dark background `#111A15`, cards `#1A2820`, text `#E8EEE8`, accent `#D8ED9C`.
- Keep text contrast high and check it before submission.

## Assets
- Group card image, tag, save button, and card text in each card component.
- Use Auto Layout for navigation, cards, form fields, and footer groups.
- Export vector icons as SVG and optimized photos as WebP.
- Use component variants for button states and light/dark themes.
